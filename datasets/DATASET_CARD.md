# Dataset Card

Full training/validation/test datasets are hosted on Hugging Face, not in
this GitHub repository, to keep the repo lightweight. Small samples (20 rows
per file) are available in `datasets/samples/` for a quick look without
downloading the full sets.

**Full datasets:** https://huggingface.co/datasets/shivateja1253/rag-pif-datasets

Includes the core train/val/test splits, the held-out evasion/adversarial/OOD
test sets, and the training data behind the v3/v4 experimental checkpoints
(for full reproducibility of the negative results discussed in the README).
